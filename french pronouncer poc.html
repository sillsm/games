This is a visual pronunciation trainer, not a full phonetic dictionary. The included examples have hand-tuned French-ish sequences; custom text uses rough spelling rules so you can see the likely mouth gestures. Use a real teacher, native speaker, or recording comparison for final correction.
What to watch: French u pulls the tongue forward while the lips round; ou rounds with the tongue farther back; nasal vowels open the nasal airflow; French r is shown as a back-of-throat/uvular gesture; f/v shows the lower lip touching the upper teeth. Slow speed exaggerates the anatomy so the motion is visible.
